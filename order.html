How to Order Online

Order your favourite Sri Lankan sweets in four easy steps. You pay by bank transfer and send us the slip on WhatsApp.

Choose your sweets and add them to the cart

Pick your sweets, or build your own gift box, then open the cart (🛒) to check everything you chose.

Start shopping

Send your order on WhatsApp

In the cart, type your name, phone number and delivery address, then press "Place order on WhatsApp". We will reply with your final total, including delivery.

Pay the amount to our bank account

Transfer the total we confirmed to the account below. Please write your name or phone number as the payment reference.

Send us the payment slip on WhatsApp

Take a photo or screenshot of your bank slip and send it to us on WhatsApp. We will confirm your order and prepare it fresh.
